ROB RUN solo money guide

Your first payday has three handoffs. Turn valuables into cash, get the money off the counter, then move it out of reach of other players.

Early Access · Rules checked November 14, 2026

Use the checklist ↓View game on Steam ↗

Your solo run checklist

Tick each step as you play. Tap a checked step to undo it.

  1. 01

    Collect valuables

    For your first solo trip, pick one sale as your goal. Find the dollar-sign van on the Looty Bay map before collecting valuables. A short trip lets you learn the handoff without risking an entire session’s haul.

  2. 02

    Collect the payout

    Sell your valuables to the fence at the marked van or at the Sales Center. Take the money from the counter immediately. Another player can steal it, and police can confiscate it. Check that you picked it up before leaving.

  3. 03

    Protect the balance

    Visit Looty Bay Bank as a Civilian to open an account. Once you have a Laundering Desk at your base, process your cash there and allow time for it to finish. Banked funds are protected from theft until the next wipe. Withdraw at an ATM when you need spending money.

Stuck between steps?

I have valuables but no base. What can I do?

You can still sell to the fence and collect cash. Lenny’s accepts cash only. A base can hold stolen goods, but other players can raid it. Until you have the desk, plan smaller trips and carry only what you can afford to lose.

Where did my sale money go?

Look back at the counter first. Completing the trade leaves the payout there for collection. Opening a bank account does not automatically transfer that payout.

Will my balance survive a wipe?

Kefir describes theft protection as lasting until the next wipe. Plan purchases around your server’s reset and check the current server schedule before saving for a large purchase.
