About Cobb Can Move

Cobb Can Move is a compact horror game built around rule changes, pressure, and simple spaces that become dangerous once Cobb gains new ways to find you. What makes Cobb Can Move memorable is how clearly the game explains the problem while still making every new round feel worse. The room stays readable, but the rules keep shifting.

This blog exists to support Cobb Can Move with short articles, strategy notes, reactions, and outside writing. If you want a place to publish thoughts on Cobb Can Move, browser horror design, survival tension, or similar indie game topics, this page is meant to make that possible without cluttering the main play pages.

The visual style stays aligned with the homepage: dark background, clean cards, sharp typography, and rounded images. The goal is simple reading, simple browsing, and a clear place where visitors can understand Cobb Can Move and submit guest post content that fits the site.

Submit A Guest Post

Good guest post topics include Cobb Can Move strategy, scene analysis, browser game recommendations, horror UI design, player reaction pieces, or comparisons with other indie games on the site.
