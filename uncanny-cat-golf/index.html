Uncanny Cat Golf FAQ

What is Uncanny Cat Golf?

Uncanny Cat Golf is a comic browser mini-golf game where a square cat becomes the ball and every course mixes readable geometry with deliberately strange internet collage.

Can I play Uncanny Cat Golf online?

Yes. Press Play above to launch the embedded browser edition without installing a separate package.

How do I aim the square cat?

Use the pointer or trackpad to set direction and force, then release the shot after checking walls, hazards, and the desired stopping area.

What is the best beginner strategy?

Plan a safe two-shot route, use controlled power, and protect the next angle instead of forcing every possible shortcut.

Can walls help with difficult holes?

Yes. Broad flat walls provide the clearest bank shots when a direct lane is blocked.

How should I improve a high score?

Replay one troublesome hole and change only the opening angle or force so the result remains easy to compare.

Does the browser game work on mobile?

It may work in a modern mobile browser, though landscape mode and desktop fullscreen make precise aiming easier.

Why is the game frame blank?

Wait for remote assets, click inside the frame, refresh once, and check extensions or networks that block the external host.

Is Uncanny Cat Golf graphic?

No graphic content appears in the supplied artwork. The game uses flashing meme-style images, uncanny faces, and absurd visual humor, though the supplied public artwork is not graphic.
